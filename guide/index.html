Start here

Launch the arcade from Play. If the frame is blocked in your browser, use the open-in-new-tab button in the player toolbar. Keep Quick Guide open on desktop, or open and close it on a phone while leaving the running game in place.

For an independent practice opening, visit Cards, choose Open Practice Pack, and use the filters on the same page to see your new discoveries. Lab packs always have three independently drawn cards and can repeat a card inside the same pack.

Card availability and the index

Use the card name to look up a Lab entry. The card sheet displays the theme, rarity, pack source, both finish values, and your copies. The full Lab catalog contains 48 currently available cards: 24 Common, 12 Rare, 8 Epic, and 4 Legendary.

The original reference index contains a Limited entry outside its regular pack pool. A listed entry may have a different acquisition rule from a regular drop. Single-player and two-player variants can share an external level identifier, so card identity is stored separately from an external reference.

Foils, slots, and target odds

Select either finish, normal only, or foil only in the calculator. The Lab foil rate is 8% per drawn card. The original reference is 10% per drawn card as of the reference date. GDGacha Arcade starter and boosted rules are handled in the game itself.

In the Lab, a specific Legendary card has a 0.75% chance in a single slot in either finish. In ten three-card Lab packs it has about a 20.2161% chance to appear at least once. Its foil version has about a 1.7844% chance in those ten packs.

Collection score and duplicates

In Lab mode one card identity contributes only its best owned finish to score. A first normal Rare contributes 30. Discovering its foil later brings that entry to 60. Another copy retains the same contribution, but increases its copy count.

The original-game scoreboard treats the first copy, subsequent copies, and foils differently. The original card reference table shows the base for one normal copy to keep this comparison clear. The arcade and Lab maintain their own collections.

Find missing cards and complete a theme

On the Cards page, select the theme you want and turn on Missing. An owned normal or foil completes one card identity. To hunt foil finishes, change the filter to No Foil. Each theme has 12 distinct entries, making the four completion goals the same size.

The rarest Lab tier contains four Legendary cards, one per theme. Opening a pack while a theme filter is selected keeps that filter available. It does not change the card pool or the odds.

Save, move, or restore a Lab collection

Your practice collection stays in this browser. Download a backup on the Cards page before moving browser profiles or devices. Import a compatible backup file and confirm the replacement after reviewing its summary.

If browser storage is unavailable, practice remains available while the page is open and a status explains temporary storage. Download a backup from that page before leaving. Arcade save and transfer options are controlled within the arcade.

Troubleshooting and version notes

Play opens the arcade only when clicked. If it remains blank, open the arcade in a separate tab. A working internet connection is needed for the remote game and videos. The guides and practice collection have a local cached path after the app finishes caching.

The original reference was last checked on November 23, 2026. The Lab rules on this site are labelled Lab v1. Card, calculator and quick-guide information share these rules. The index can show blank results when filters are too narrow; clear the search and filters to see every Lab card.
