What Apex means

Ranks 1 contain 1 regular cards. Any Apex card accounts for 0.00423% of a single regular slot, counting both finishes. A first normal copy is worth 10,000 points.

Use either finish when you are looking for a first copy. Switch to foil only to see the much smaller chance of a Holo GRIEF. Opening more packs increases the number of independent chances.
