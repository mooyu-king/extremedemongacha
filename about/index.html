What we make

Demon Pack Lab is an independent browser guide and card collection. The site presents the GDGacha arcade alongside short explanations of opening packs, finding odds, and managing a collection.

How to read our data

We date the public original-game reference separately from Lab v1 and the embedded GDGacha Arcade. The 48 Lab practice cards and visual illustrations are original guide materials. The arcade and linked walkthroughs are operated by their respective providers.

Corrections
