About Eyes in the Water

Eyes in the Water appears at rank #51 in the original reference snapshot, in the Epic tier. Its tier covers ranks 36–100 and contains 65 regular drops in that snapshot. The listed level ID is 95851008. The linked video is credited to LordVadercraft.

How the score works

A first normal Eyes in the Water is worth 360 points and a first foil is worth 1,080 points. These are the original game’s card points. Later copies contribute less, and foil copies are counted first.
