Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026.

Browser storage

Practice cards, favorites and settings are stored on this device for the Lab. Backup files stay with you when you download them. Import restores the local collection from your selected file. We do not provide a Lab account or cloud backup.

Statistics and caching

We use a Plausible analytics script at data.1back.link for aggregate site activity. Analytics requests may contain normal network and browser information. Lab save contents are not included. The app can cache same-origin pages and assets in your browser to speed up return visits.

Arcade and videos

Playing the GDGacha arcade or loading a YouTube video makes a request to those providers. They may use their own browser storage, analytics, ads, and privacy settings. Use their published policies to learn about those services.
