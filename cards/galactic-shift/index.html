About Galactic Shift

Galactic Shift appears at rank #646 in the original reference snapshot, in the Common tier. Its tier covers ranks 641–1,638 and contains 998 regular drops in that snapshot. The listed level ID is 59829005. The linked video is credited to jbmoon.

How the score works

A first normal Galactic Shift is worth 12 points and a first foil is worth 36 points. These are the original game’s card points. Later copies contribute less, and foil copies are counted first.
