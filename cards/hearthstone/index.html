About Hearthstone

Hearthstone is a Common card in the Ember theme. It is available in the site's original Lab Practice pack, where each pack draws 3 cards. The preview shows this card's own illustration and base rules. The warm center of the six Ember commons.

How the score works

A first normal Hearthstone is worth 10 points and a first foil is worth 20 points. Lab Practice counts the best finish you own for each card. Extra copies increase your copy count and leave the card’s score unchanged.

An owned copy counts as one entry toward its theme, even if you later pull the foil. Use the odds below to decide how many packs to try in Lab Practice.

Hearthstone Drop Odds
